HOW TO PLAY
Viruses are loose in the dish! Press and hold anywhere to blow a bubble — let go, and anything caught inside is stuck. Keep a virus bubbled for 3 seconds to cure it.
Every bubble drains your antidote, and a virus touching a growing bubble costs a life. Clear the dish to advance. That’s it — go save the petri dish.

SPACING · RADII · STROKES
Spacing scale (units): 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64. Grid cell 40. Safe inset for interactive controls: 24 from canvas edges.

Strokes: 1 hairlines · 2 arena, bubble, meter track, icon rings · 3 cure-timer arc, hint rings.

RATIONALE — canvas & rails
1280×720: exact 16:9 in integers, divisible by the 40-unit grid and the 4-unit spacing base, and maps 1:1 to 720p / 2:1 to 1440p so strokes stay crisp at common window sizes.
120 / 1040 / 120 split: a 120-unit rail is the minimum that fits a 56-unit touch target with 24-unit insets on both sides (24+56+24=104) plus breathing room; the playfield gains ~30% area over 800×600 while keeping shapes round. Arena inset 12, stroke 2, radius 20 → live area 1016×696.
Desktop vs phone: identical layout — the canvas scales uniformly. On 19.5:9–20:9 phones the 16:9 canvas letterboxes ~40–60 physical px per side; rail controls sit ≥24 units inside the canvas, so even with OS insets they clear notches and corner radii. Hover states are desktop-only; pressed states are shared. Pause top-left and fullscreen top-right land directly under thumbs resting on the phone's short edges in landscape.
Teaching without words: bubble and meter share the lime "antidote" color, so draining reads as cause→effect; the cure timer is a depleting arc on the bubble itself; the first-run overlay (1j) reinforces all three once.
ANIMATION NOTES — all transform/alpha only
Menu in: logo scales 0.92→1 with overshoot cubic-bezier(0.34,1.56,0.64,1) 320ms; buttons/panels fade+rise 12u, 40ms stagger. The logo's O-bubble drifts ±3u vertically, 3s sine loop; its trapped virus wiggles ±4° at 1.5s.
Overlays (pause, level clear, game over, help, file): scrim alpha 0→target 160ms ease-out; panel scale 0.94→1 + overshoot 220ms; exit 120ms ease-in to 0.96, scrim out 140ms. Pause must feel instant: gameplay halts frame 1, visuals catch up.
Antidote bar: drain tracks value with 90ms linear smoothing; refills ease-out 300ms; color crossfades 150ms at 34%/67% thresholds; below 25% the fill pulses alpha 1→0.6→1 at 800ms and the % label turns coral.
Score: counts up over 240ms (ease-out); on change digits pop scale 1→1.18→1 in 100ms. Cure awards spawn a floating "+250" at the pop, rising 40u fading 600ms.
New best: at the moment score passes best mid-run, BEST value flips gold with a 1→1.3→1 pop; on game over, gold rings expand 0→560/700u fading 500ms, confetti (24 rects, gravity ~900u/s², spin ±540°/s) for 1.2s.
Life lost: coral vignette alpha 0→0.26→0 over 400ms; "−1 LIFE" floats up 60u fading 900ms from the death point; the lost pip scales 1→1.6 fading 300ms.
Cure timer: arc sweeps linearly; final second, arc + bubble stroke pulse at 4Hz; on cure, bubble scales 1→1.15 then pops to 12 particles (350ms).
